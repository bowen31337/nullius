## What was learned (reported, not decisive)

- **The defect.** `_metrics_horizon` = shortest covered horizon = 1 for every campaign ever run; the "native-horizon OOS" feature was a no-op. The six earlier M2 RESULTs, the M3 dreaming RESULT and the h5 re-pricing RESULT all describe one-day figures as horizon-5 or horizon-10 figures. Their numbers stand; their horizon labels do not. See Addendum 2.
- **Campaign 1's pick** is a one-day momentum construct whose one-day out-of-sample return is negative. Its genuine 5-day in-sample reading (the decile diagnostic, 183 dates) is IC −0.011 (t −0.8), top-minus-bottom decile −24.7 bp per 5 days: the one-day winner is not a five-day signal.
- **Attention-volume roots** (three, campaign 1) rank the cross-section at 5 days (rank-IC t +2.1 to +3.1 on 61 dates) with the gradient in the bottom decile (−41 to −54 bp per 5 days versus the cross-section) and nothing in the top decile; no decile book on them reaches t 1.4 at this sample size. This is the construction question the next pre-registration should state up front: short-bottom-decile against the rest, not top-minus-bottom.
- **Root diversity.** Three basis-premium roots scored identically from different code; a third of that theme's workspace budget bought one construct.
