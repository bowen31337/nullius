## The null defect

Found 2026-10-21 at the verdict, by reading the close-out's `charges_budget` flags; proved read-only the same morning (scratchpad scripts `carry_leak_check.py`, `null_carry_sweep.py`; nothing on `main` edited).

- **Mechanism.** Under `instrument: perp` (commit `c0e3c1b5`, 2026-10-14) the per-symbol return the metrics reduce is `price return − fee − funding carry`, the carry read from the real symbol over the holding days (`orchestrator/_evaluate.py`, the `charges()` closure). The null branch permutes the price return across symbols within a date and leaves the carry on its original symbol. A signal that is a function of funding or basis correlates with its own unpermuted carry: the funding-crowding picks' signals have rank-IC +0.53 (t 27) against the carry alone.
- **Size.** Scoring each node's signal against 100 fresh derangements on 61 dates: under the clean construction (permuted price only) the null t is centred at −0.05 with sd 1 and 2 to 4 % above 1.96; under the chain's actual construction it is centred at **+1.78, +1.15 and +1.42** (nodes `1e00d94c`, `f3896a87`, `69403eb3`) with **39 %, 19 % and 31 %** above 1.96. On the full 183-date grid the shift is about √3 larger. Low-volatility, momentum and attention nulls are unaffected (their signals do not track funding); the five low-volatility nulls here all sit at |t| < 1.5 while real low-volatility roots score t 5 to 7.
- **Proof that the nulls were permuted, not served real targets.** The campaign-1 real pick's stored IC series reproduces against real net returns at correlation 1.000; the campaign-7 null pick's stored series correlates at −0.14 to −0.16 with its signal's IC against real returns at either horizon.
- **The commit rule ranks nulls.** `closeout._commit_top_node` takes the highest `ir_tstat` over every evaluated node and never reads the null bit, so campaigns 7 and 10 committed null branches. These are the only two null commits among the 42 `m2-fixed` rows ever written, because before the perp instrument the null reference was unbiased and a null rarely reached the bar.
- **What this invalidates.** For every perpetual-instrument campaign since 2026-10-14 (the 18 campaigns of `2026-10-14-m2-perp-beta-neutral`, the one campaign of `2026-10-19-m2-wide-h5`, these ten), KS gate A, FDR_deploy, sensitivity and specificity compared real nodes against a biased null for the funding and basis themes; campaign 2's VOID here was this bias, not a rare draw, and the operator's 2026-10-20 "null validity resolved" note was wrong because its check used a bare price panel rather than the chain's post-cost target. Real nodes' own scores are not wrong (the carry is real P&L); only the null reference is. The two forward shadows (v1, paper) hold real nodes selected on in-sample scores; their values are unchanged, and their P&L is forward market return.
- **Fix (to be dispatched after this RESULT, through claw-forge, with the suites and the canary as gates):** permute the carry with the same derangement as the price return, so a null target is `r_π(i) − fee − carry_π(i)`; make the commit rule skip `charges_budget: false` nodes; add a null-power check that runs a funding-rank signal through the chain's own post-cost target. Until that lands, no perpetual campaign's calibration verdict is trustworthy for derivatives-input themes.

## What was learned (reported, not decisive)

- **At the true horizon the ranking is real and repeatable.** Out-of-sample rank-IC t is 2.15 (funding-crowding), 2.72 and 2.85 (low-volatility) and 1.70 (basis-premium) on the real picks; low-volatility scores IC t 5 to 7 in sample in every campaign. v1's one-day numbers never showed this.
- **A tail book captures a third of it.** The best constructions reach t 1.8 to 1.9 on 60 dates; the mean is a basis-premium effect that is largely funding carry on shorted crowded coins, a known and cheap effect rather than discovered alpha.
- **The short leg carries the gradient**, in all six profiles. A dollar-neutral top-minus-bottom book pays for a long leg that does nothing.
- **The IR t bar at 2.0 sits on the constructs' edge.** Abstentions came at 1.97, 1.99 and 1.79; commits at 2.06 to 2.79. With a biased null in two themes the bar's false-discovery control is unknown there.
