# Addendum 2 to the M2-wide-h5 pre-registration: the series was stopped after one campaign because the engine never scored at horizon 5

- **Filed:** 2026-10-20 03:55 AEDT (2026-10-19 16:55 UTC), after campaign 1 (`870d4594`) closed out at 16:09:33 UTC and while campaign 2 (`2f4630c5`) was planting roots. `2026-10-19-m2-wide-h5.md` is unchanged and its `.sha256` still verifies. The series driver and campaign 2 were stopped by PID at 16:55:44 UTC; campaign 2 had planted 18 roots, evaluated none, and wrote no `m2-fixed` row.
- **What was found.** The pre-registration says the grid is "horizon 5" and that every pick is "selected for" and "judged at native horizon 5". Neither is true of the engine, and never was. `evaluator._metrics._metrics_horizon` scores **the shortest horizon the priced panel covers**; both evaluation chains align every horizon the spec names (1, 2, 5, 10, 20) and both oracles answer every one of them, so every node metric the system has ever stored (`ic_mean`, `ic_tstat`, `ir_standalone`, `ir_tstat`, `cost_adjusted_ir`, `ic_series.parquet`, the tripwire panel) and every out-of-sample reading (`m2-fixed` rows, `portfolio-oos`) is a **one-day-forward** figure, with the full rebalance fee charged on each weekly or biweekly date. The config's `horizon` reaches only the campaign row, the shadow book's holding time, and a fallback in the forward-return oracle that the gate never exercises. Feature 5 of `additions_spec_engine_edge_recovery.xml` ("native-horizon OOS scoring", `a5c5a7bf`) re-horizoned the context to no effect.
- **Proof.** Node `886a711a` (campaign 1's committed pick): the Spearman of its normalized scores against the 1-day forward return reproduces its stored `ic_series` with correlation 0.9998 over 61 dates (the residual is the post-cost adjustment); against the 5-day return the correlation is 0.49 and the mean IC flips sign (+0.0073 stored versus −0.0279 at 5 days). Scripts: `scratchpad/horizon_check.py`, `scratchpad/oos_horizon_check.py` (session 716fb101); the reproduction is pinned in the bug spec below.
- **Why stop rather than finish.** The registered hypothesis is a five-day weekly tail-decile book at maker fees. The engine was measuring and selecting a one-day book that pays a week's fee per day of return, an objective nobody registered. Nine more campaigns (about 27 hours) would have produced a verdict about that objective, not about the hypothesis, so the primary outcome is **not computed** and this test is recorded as **VOID (measurement defect)**, not as a pass or a fail of the space. The ≥ 4-campaign floor in the rule would in any case not have been met.
- **What is kept.** Campaign 1's close-out is on record exactly as written (calibrated, FDR_deploy 0.0, KS p 0.37, 39 nodes, pick `886a711a` intermediate-momentum depth 1, in-sample +22.9 bp per **day**, out-of-sample −13.5 bp per **day** under the registered config). Its `m2-fixed` row is a horizon-1 reading and is not rewritten. Nothing of campaign 2 is scored.
- **What follows.** (1) The fix is `bug_spec_metrics_horizon.xml` (two bugs: a keyword-only `horizon=` on `compute_node_metrics`, and an evaluation-config key `metrics_horizon` ∈ {"shortest", "configured"}, default "shortest" so every stored number and the evaluation canary keep their meaning). (2) Every earlier RESULT that describes a metric as "at horizon 5" or "at horizon 10" (`2026-10-17-m3-dreaming.RESULT.md`, `2026-10-19-h5-repricing.RESULT.md`, the six M2 RESULTs) gets one erratum line pointing here; the numbers stand as one-day figures. (3) The series is re-registered as a new pre-registration under a config that sets `metrics_horizon: "configured"`, after the fix merges and a two-pick equality gate shows the default path unchanged and the configured path at horizon 5. No campaign runs before that.
- **Secondary observation, not decisive.** The decile diagnostic on campaign 1 (five nodes, 61 in-sample dates, genuine 5-day returns): the three attention-volume roots rank the cross-section (rank-IC t +2.1 to +3.1, carried by the tails) but no decile book reaches t 1.4; the bottom decile lags by 41 to 54 bp per 5 days on all three, the top decile adds nothing. `scratchpad/c1_decile_diag.json`.
- **Out-of-sample proof, added 04:03 AEDT.** `evaluate_on_dates` on node `886a711a` over the 60 out-of-sample dates under the registered config with the chain's own forward-return oracle returns `NodeMetrics.horizon == 1` and cost-adjusted IR **−0.001348**, the stored `m2-fixed` value to the digit (`scratchpad/oos_horizon_check.log`). An oracle narrowed by hand to horizon 5 is refused by the gate's substitution guard, so no stored out-of-sample reading can have been anything but horizon 1.
