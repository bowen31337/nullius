## Diagnostic (Addendum 3, not a criterion)
The unrevised incumbent against `m2-incumbent`, over the same 103 worlds: mean ΔIR **−0.0000180** (two-sided p 0.716), commit rate 0.757. So the policy family alone is indistinguishable from M2, and dreaming moved it by about +0.000034, also indistinguishable from zero. Neither the new family nor its revision improves on the M2 fixed rule out of sample.

## What the result says
What dreaming learned on the bootstrap pool is real there, and it transfers between bootstrap domains. It does not transfer to financial worlds. The one parameter selection moved, the commit bar, is the one that touches the financial arm, and lowering it made the policy commit more often to signals whose in-sample standing did not survive out of sample. That is the same reversal the M2 tests found (in-sample t > 2 with negative OOS), seen here as a rise in FDR_deploy.

## Erratum (filed 2026-10-19, after the verdict; the verdict stands as written)
The pre-registration admits "every completed, non-VOID campaign" as a financial world and does not check the campaign's own in-sample grid against the OOS grid. Of the 103 admitted worlds, **17 were not out of sample for the committed picks**:
- 7 campaigns (1ffef8b3, 90d95c6d, 9a955be6, bb0f1919, bf02fefe, 4bf280b8, a9656ef3; created 2026-10-07/08) ran on a 92-day *daily* grid ending **2026-09-30**, inside the OOS window.
- 6 campaigns (36bb6e55, 6284e1bd, 65fe5d49, 6db69c83, d511433c, e1947ec8; created 2026-10-08/09) ran on the 235-date weekly grid ending **2026-06-29**, overlapping 22 of the 30 OOS dates.
- 4 smoke campaigns (2a0700fa, 55b317ef, 87bfda23, efe1aff2) have no node artifacts to classify; no arm committed on them.

The remaining **86 worlds** (56 on the 92-date biweekly grid and 30 on the 183/180-date weekly grids, all ending 2025-06-30) are clean. Recomputed on those 86 alone, with misses at 0.0: `m2-incumbent` −3.22 bp per period (commit rate 0.37), the unrevised incumbent −3.45 bp (0.83), the winner −3.36 bp (0.91). The paired difference stays indistinguishable from zero and the FAIL holds. The overlap could only have flattered both arms, so it does not weaken the verdict; it is recorded because a future pre-registration must exclude such campaigns by rule, and because the M2 RESULT files' per-campaign OOS figures for the 13 campaigns above are likewise not out of sample.

Also recorded: the score field is the mean per-date post-cost return of the node's dollar-neutral book (basis points per 10-day period), not an information ratio, so criterion 1's "> 0.3" threshold, copied from the PRD, could not bind on this field. The criterion still failed on its significance test and on the selection bar, so this did not decide the verdict either.
