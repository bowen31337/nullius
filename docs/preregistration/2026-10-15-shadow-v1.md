The venue is BingX VST, with simulated funds: **no capital is at risk, and nothing here moves capital.** Any step toward live capital (M5) needs the owner's explicit approval, whatever this test shows.

**Book.** This is the research book v2 exactly: each node's z-scores are turned into `evaluator.book_weights`, with dollar-neutral, gross-1 legs rescaled to equal 60-day BTC beta. Specifically:
- The five nodes' books are combined by equal-weight mean, then renormalised to gross 1.0.
- Symbols are restricted to those listed as BingX VST contracts, with matching prices: a contract more than 2× from the lake's perp close is excluded.
- `equity_usdt` is 10000.
- Config `evaluation-config-archive-perp-bn.json` (sha256 `6adc9a93…6425`): instrument perp, horizon 10, seed 20261006. Fees `binance_usdm.yaml` (sha256 `2183ec64…0ba8`).

**Cadence.** There is one decision date D every 14 days on the research grid (anchor 2022-01-03). Each book is computed at 02:30 UTC on D+1 from data through D, and traded from the 04:05 UTC slot on D+1. That is one day after the research convention, which enters at D's close; the lag is disclosed. Between decision dates the target weights are held, and the 4-hour slots re-place toward them. The first decision date is **2026-10-19**.

**Switchover.** `deploy/shadow/shadow_switch.sh` runs once, when the first book with D ≥ 2026-10-19 exists. It closes the fixture positions with `vst-flatten --confirm`, then repoints the rebalance unit's `active_book.json` at the shadow book.

## Window
The window runs for **90 calendar days, from the switch slot (expected 2026-10-20 04:05 UTC) to 2027-01-18 04:05 UTC.** That covers about 7 decision dates (2026-10-19 to 2027-01-04).

## Primary outcome (decided now)
**P (paper, research accounting).** For each day d in the window:
- r_d = Σ_i w_{i,d} · (fut_close_{i,d} / fut_close_{i,d−1} − 1) − Σ_i w_{i,d} · funding_rate_sum_{i,d} − 0.0005 · Σ_i |w_{i,d} − w_{i,d−1}|
- w_{i,d} is the book in force on day d, from `~/.local/share/nullius/shadow/history/`.
- Closes and funding come from the shadow lake.

**Pass** requires both:
- the cumulative net return Σ r_d > 0
- the daily IR t-stat, mean(r)/sd(r)·√n, of at least 1.0

Otherwise the test **fails**.

A t of 1.0 is a low bar, chosen honestly. 90 days and about 7 rebalances give little power, so this test mainly guards against a clearly negative forward result. It cannot establish a strong edge on its own.

**Execution validity.** Required for a pass to count toward M4:
- every decision date in the window produced a book, and the VST bot traded it
- at least 90% of the target legs over the window were placed, from `vst.db` placements

A harness defect (a missing book, two or more failed slots in a row) is recorded in an ADDENDUM, and the window is extended by the lost days. It is never scored as a pass.

## Secondary (reported, not decisive)
- the realised VST account P&L against P
- fill fidelity (`router.vst_fidelity`)
- forward IC per decision date and per node, against the next 10 days' perp returns
- the funding share of the return
- the realised book beta
- turnover
- the drawdown path
- the daily-loss halts that fired

## Rules during the window
- Nodes, weights, cadence and cost assumptions do not change. A code change that alters the book's values counts as a deviation and is recorded.
- The daily-loss guard stays on. A halt is a recorded outcome, not a reason to stop.
- There is no early stop on performance.

## Outcome actions
- **Pass, and valid:** M4's criteria are met for this book. It is reported to the owner, and any M5 step waits for the owner's explicit approval.
- **Fail:** the nodes are demoted, and their forward decay is recorded (β₄). Research continues.
