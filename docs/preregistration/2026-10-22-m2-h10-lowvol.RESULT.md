## What was learned (reported, not decisive)

- **Horizon 10 did what the sweep said in sample and not out of sample.** In-sample IR t rose to 2.3 to 3.8 (from 1.4 to 2.3 at horizon 5 for the sweep's nodes); out of sample the same construction lost 40 bp a period. At horizon 5 the picks' rank-IC t is 3.4 to 5.0 out of sample and their books are flat; at horizon 10 the rank-IC t is 1.5 to 2.8 and the books lose. Whatever the hold, the book's return is decided by the shorted decile's tail, not by the ranking's strength.
- **Rank-IC does not price the short leg's tail.** Ten of ten picks rank the forward cross-section; the book's return is set by a handful of squeeze dates in the shorted decile. A dollar-neutral book that is short high-volatility names is short volatility-of-volatility, and no ranking statistic sees that.
- **The agent's search is one idea deep.** Given one theme, 27 roots and three rounds produced residual volatility, range volatility and lottery-payoff proxies, all monotone transforms of "how much does this coin move". Funding dispersion (campaign 3) was the only construct outside that family and the only one with a weak ranking.
