# Pre-registration: M2 on the wide perpetual universe at horizon 5, scored as a portfolio of tail-decile books (M2-wide-h5)

- **Registered:** 2026-10-20 00:20 AEDT (2026-10-19 13:20 UTC), before any campaign below runs and before any node of this series exists. `main` is at `d795e929` (the tail-decile book `1e49d89a`, the point-in-time repair of the panel cache `1c138783` and its performance fix `d795e929` merged; every default path unchanged, enforced by the nightly evaluation canary).
- **Why:** seven M2 tests and M3 failed on one engine configuration: 85–100 names, horizon 10 biweekly (or 5 weekly at spot taker fees), a book proportional to score. `docs/research/2026-10-19-alpha-diagnosis.md` and `2026-10-19-h5-repricing.RESULT.md` then established, on stored picks and without new hypotheses:
  - breadth works on the ranking (85 → 239 names took the horizon-5 picks' median out-of-sample IC t from +0.45 to +1.92; the portfolio's rank IC t was +2.45),
  - fees were the whole of the proportional book's loss (gross +0.4 bp per period at zero fees),
  - and a proportional dollar-neutral book does not convert that ranking into return, because in-sample the return gradient sits in the **bottom decile** (−63 to −73 bp per 5-day period for the volatility and borrow-proxy constructs) while the other nine deciles are flat and non-monotone; the top-minus-bottom spread was +126 to +136 bp (t 2.0–2.4 over 61 in-sample weekly dates) where the proportional book earned +6 to +27 bp.
  This series changes the space along exactly those three axes and nothing else: the universe, the fee mode, and the book construction. The hypothesis space (the nine owner-approved v2 themes), the fixed selection rule, the planted nulls and the calibration gates are unchanged. PRD §2.3: a space pivot is recorded with its reason; this is it.
- **What this is not:** no dreaming (M3 is FAIL, final). No capital. The archive OOS window is a screen, not a proof (below).

## Design
- **10 Type-R campaigns**, `wideH5-01` to `wideH5-10`, each `--workspaces 27 --rounds 3 --width 4`, null fraction 0.15, run one after another by `~/.local/share/nullius/ingest-logs/m2_wide_h5_series.sh`. - **Selection** is the M2 fixed rule at close-out, unchanged: among a campaign's nodes, net `ir_tstat` ≥ 2.0 with positive IC, ranked by `ir_tstat`, ties by node id; otherwise the campaign abstains. Each close-out writes the pick's `m2-fixed` row under this config at horizon 5.
- **Null-calibration gate** as in every M2 test: a campaign whose KS test on null versus real in-sample scores has p < 0.05 is VOID and excluded. Tripwires are advisory at discovery.

## Primary outcome (decided now)
Over the non-VOID campaigns of the series, take every committed pick (a campaign that abstains contributes nothing) and score them as **one equal-weight portfolio of their tail-decile books** over the 60 out-of-sample dates with `./run.sh portfolio-oos --picks <the series' picks>` under this config (native horizon 5, maker, tail_decile). **Pass** iff the portfolio's mean net return is > 0 **and** its t-statistic mean/sd·√60 is ≥ 2.0 **and** at least 4 campaigns committed. **Fail** otherwise. One computation; the first complete run after the tenth campaign's close-out is the verdict.

## Secondary (reported, not decisive)
- The same picks under `inverse_vol` and under `z` weighting (two sibling configs, differing only in `book.weighting`), so the construction effect is shown on the series' own picks.
- The classic per-campaign rule (median `oos_ir` > 0 and ≥ 6 positive picks), for continuity with the six earlier RESULT files.
- Per theme: in-sample IC t ≥ 2 pass rate for real versus planted-null nodes; committed picks; out-of-sample IC t and net return. Gross versus fees (a zero-fee companion run). N_eff and mean pairwise correlation of the picks. VOID and insufficient rates, gate p-values, spend and wall-clock.
- The in-sample decile profile of each committed pick (the diagnostic above, on the pick's own in-sample dates), so a pass or fail can be read against where the gradient was.

## What follows
- **Pass:** the committed portfolio is promoted to a **paper-only forward book** (a sibling of the attention paper track, pre-registered before any forward data exists) and, if that passes, to a VST shadow. No capital question arises before the forward test.
- **Fail:** the wide-universe, horizon-5, tail-decile space is recorded as not producing a monetisable committed portfolio under the fixed rule. Per PRD §2.3 the next lever is inputs or horizon, not search, and it is pre-registered then.
