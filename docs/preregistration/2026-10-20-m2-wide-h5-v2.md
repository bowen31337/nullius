## Primary outcome (decided now)
Over the non-VOID campaigns, every committed pick scored as **one equal-weight portfolio of their tail-decile books** over the 60 out-of-sample dates with `./run.sh portfolio-oos --picks <the series' picks>` under this config (horizon 5, maker, tail_decile). **Pass** iff the portfolio's mean net 5-day return is > 0 **and** mean/sd·√60 ≥ 2.0 **and** at least 4 campaigns committed. **Fail** otherwise. One computation; the first complete run after the tenth close-out is the verdict. A series stopped before the tenth close-out for any reason other than a measurement defect is scored on the campaigns that closed out, with the 4-campaign floor still binding.

## Secondary (reported, not decisive)
- The same picks under `inverse_vol` (`evaluation-config-wide-h5-maker-iv-mh.json`) and `z` (`evaluation-config-wide-h5-maker-z-mh.json`) weighting, both at the configured horizon, so the construction effect is shown on the series' own picks.
- The classic per-campaign rule (median `oos_ir` > 0 and ≥ 6 positive picks), for continuity.
- Per theme: in-sample IC t ≥ 2 pass rate for real versus planted-null nodes; committed picks; out-of-sample IC t and net return. Gross versus fees (a zero-fee companion). N_eff and mean pairwise correlation. VOID and insufficient rates, gate p-values, spend and wall-clock.
- **The decile profile of every committed pick** on its own in-sample dates at the chain's 5-day returns, reporting top-minus-bottom, rest-minus-bottom and middle-minus-bottom. v1's campaign 1 showed, on three attention-volume roots at genuine 5-day returns (61 dates), the gradient entirely in the bottom decile (−41 to −54 bp per 5 days against the cross-section) and nothing in the top decile; no decile book reached t 1.4 at that sample. Whether a short-bottom-decile-against-the-rest book beats top-minus-bottom is the construction question this reports on and does **not** decide; a book of that shape is not in the engine and is not used here.
