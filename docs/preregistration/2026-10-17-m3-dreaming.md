# Pre-registration: M3, the dreaming gate

- **Filed:** 2026-10-17, before any dreaming cycle has run on the research store (`policy_revision` holds 0 rows, and `replay_score` holds only the 84 `m2-fixed` baseline rows written by close-out).
- **Question:** can a search-and-select policy, revised offline over stored worlds, commit better picks than the M2 fixed baseline on financial worlds it was never selected on? This is the thesis PRD §12 M3 tests, and its criteria are copied here unchanged. This document fixes the parts the PRD leaves open: what a non-committing world scores, which worlds count, how each criterion is computed, and when the verdict is final.

## Worlds

- **Bootstrap pool (selection only).** At least **50** bootstrap worlds, drawn from all three generated domains (`hpo`, `featsel`, `symreg`) with at least **10** worlds per domain. Below 50 the verdict is refused, not computed. Dreaming cycles may still run at 20 or more worlds (the ladder floor), but no such cycle can produce an M3 verdict.
- **Financial worlds (judgement only).** Every completed, non-`VOID` campaign admitted by `discovery.admit_completed_campaigns`, on the evaluation config's out-of-sample grid. They are never used to select a policy; every candidate is chosen on bootstrap train worlds alone. That makes the whole financial set the M3 holdout ("financial holdout worlds", architecture §10.6). At least **20** must be admitted, or the verdict is refused.
- The verdict reads the pool **as it stands when the verdict runs**. Its world ids, counts per domain and per-campaign `campaign_id`s are written into the verdict row.

## The incumbent

The first incumbent is the **M2 fixed baseline written in the `question.*` policy contract**, not an independently written policy, so that the comparison changes one thing: dreaming. Before the first cycle, that incumbent is replayed over every admitted financial world. It must reproduce close-out's `m2-fixed` committed pick (or miss) on every world, and its score to within 1e-12. If it does not, no cycle runs until the difference is explained and fixed.

## A world where a policy commits nothing

A policy that commits no pick on a world takes no position there. For every paired statistic in this document, **a non-committing world scores an IR of exactly 0.0**: a flat book earns nothing and pays no cost. This is the rule the M2 RESULT files already used ("misses counted as 0"). The stored score stays `-inf` (`NON_COMMITTING_SCORE`); only the statistic maps it to 0.0. The commit rate of each arm is reported beside every criterion, but does not decide any.

## Procedure

1. One dreaming cycle (`python -m orchestrator.dream`), with the reviser `jitter`, the revision cap from the cycle-cap schedule (`M = 10` below 50 worlds, `M = 40` at 50 or more), and `beta = 0.5`. Every candidate is swept over the bootstrap train worlds, the argmax on train is the winner, and both the winner and the incumbent are swept over the bootstrap holdout worlds.
2. The winner and the incumbent are replayed over every admitted financial world, under the identical replay path close-out's own commit uses.
3. `python -m orchestrator.m3_verdict --iteration-id ID` computes the three criteria below once and writes one row.

## The three criteria (all three must pass)

1. **Paired ΔIR.** Over all admitted financial worlds, the winner's committed-pick OOS IR minus `m2-fixed`'s, paired by world, with non-committing worlds at 0.0: the mean difference is **> 0.3**, a one-sided paired test gives **p < 0.05**, and the difference clears the meta-level selection bar `√(2 ln M) · σ / √n` at the cycle's recorded `M` (`dreaming.cycle_bar`).
2. **`FDR_deploy` improves.** `FDR_deploy` at π₀ = 0.9, from the planted-null labels of the picks each arm committed across the admitted campaigns (computed in the scorer process, the only process that may join null labels), is **strictly lower** for the winner than for `m2-fixed`.
3. **Leave-one-family-out transfer > 0.** For each bootstrap domain d in turn, the argmax among the cycle's own candidates is retaken on the train worlds of the other two domains only. Its score minus the incumbent's, averaged over every world of domain d, is that domain's transfer. The criterion is that the **mean over the three domains is > 0**.

## When the verdict is final

- One verdict per pre-registered attempt. The verdict row carries this document's sha256. A second verdict under the same attempt is refused, whatever its parameters.
- A **FAIL is final for this space**. Do not rerun with other parameters, and do not proceed to M4 on a dreamed policy. Per PRD §2.3 the effort moves to hypothesis-space breadth. Reopening dreaming needs a new pre-registration on a materially different space.
- Any fix to the computation after a cycle has run is filed as an ADDENDUM before the verdict runs. If a verdict has already been written, it stands as written.
