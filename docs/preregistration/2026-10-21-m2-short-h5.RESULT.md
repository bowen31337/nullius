## What was learned (reported, not decisive)

- **The in-sample carry on crowded coins did not persist.** All ten short-tail books of persistently high-funding coins earned +42 to +51 bp per period in sample (2022-01 to 2025-06) and lost 4 to 32 bp per period out of sample (2025-08 to 2026-09). The out-of-sample rank-IC t of the same picks is −0.83 to +0.58: the signal does not rank the forward cross-section in this window either. This is the funding-carry regime change, not a construction artefact: v2's funding-crowding picks were the weakest theme out of sample as well (−9.3 bp under tail-decile).
- **The short-tail book answered the construction question in the negative.** v2's decile profiles put the whole gradient in the bottom decile; shorting that decile against the rest raised the in-sample IR t of one theme by 1.5 to 2 units and left the out-of-sample reading negative. Under the same ten picks, tail-decile loses −32.6 bp and inverse-vol earns +2.6 bp; under v2's six picks short-tail earns +9.0 bp against tail-decile's +22.2 bp. The construction moves the number by tens of basis points in either direction and no construction reaches t 2 on either pick set. Reading: the construction question is closed for this space and window; the remaining variable is what is ranked, not how the ranking is traded.
- **Root diversity is zero within a theme.** Twenty-seven workspaces per campaign produced, for funding-crowding, the same construct from different source text, with statistics identical to four decimals in two campaigns. The policy's exploration is over wording, not over hypotheses; a series of ten campaigns sampled one book ten times.
