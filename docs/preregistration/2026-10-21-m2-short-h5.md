# Pre-registration: M2 on the wide perpetual universe at horizon 5, scored as a portfolio of short-tail books (M2-short-h5)

- **Registered:** 2026-10-21 03:38:34 UTC (the registering commit's own timestamp), before any campaign below runs and before any node of this series exists. `main` is at `551cdbaf` (`d2838556` the funded return as the null-permuted target; `95a7b352` the commit rule skips null branches; `ac6378f8` the null-power check on the chain's own target; `26d26843`/`08028f18` the VST slot fixes; `b00047b2` the `short_tail` weighting; every default path unchanged — evaluation canary deviation 0.0 after each).
- **Why this series.** `2026-10-20-m2-wide-h5-v2.RESULT.md` (FAIL, t 1.23) found, at the true 5-day horizon, that the committed constructs rank the cross-section out of sample (rank-IC t 2.2 to 2.9 on the real picks) while a long-top-decile / short-bottom-decile book captures about a third of it. Every decile profile in v1 and v2 (twelve picks, three themes) puts the return gradient in the bottom decile and shows no gradient in the top decile. This registration changes exactly one thing about the book: it shorts the bottom decile and goes long every other scored name equal-weight, so the long leg is the market-like complement rather than a second bet on a decile that carries nothing. It also narrows the hypothesis space to the three themes that produced every discovery in v1 and v2, and it is the first series whose planted nulls are valid for the funding and basis themes (the RESULT's "null defect", fixed `d2838556`).
- **What this is not:** no dreaming (M3 is FAIL, final). No capital. The archive OOS window is a screen, not a proof (twelfth look, below).

## Primary outcome (decided now)
Over the non-VOID campaigns, every committed pick scored as **one equal-weight portfolio of their short-tail books** over the 60 out-of-sample dates with `./run.sh portfolio-oos --picks <the series' picks>` under this config. **Pass** iff the portfolio's mean net 5-day return is > 0 **and** mean/sd·√60 ≥ 2.0 **and** at least 4 campaigns committed. **Fail** otherwise. One computation; the first complete run after the tenth close-out is the verdict. A series stopped before the tenth close-out for any reason other than a measurement defect is scored on the campaigns that closed out, with the 4-campaign floor still binding.
