- **Registered:** 2026-10-22 10:27:12 UTC, before any campaign below runs and before any node of this series exists. `main` is at `418ab4d8` (`418ab4d8` the `dedup_committed_constructs` selection variant, default off; every earlier default path unchanged; evaluation canary deviation 0.0 after the merge).
- **Why this series.** Three series at horizon 5 on this universe are closed: `2026-10-19-m2-wide-h5` (VOID), `2026-10-20-m2-wide-h5-v2` (FAIL, t 1.23) and `2026-10-21-m2-short-h5` (FAIL, t −0.84). Second, a ten-campaign series under one config commits one construct ten times (short-h5: pairwise correlation 0.91, two picks identical to four decimals), so the portfolio verdict measured one book, not a space. This registration changes three things, each named by the short-h5 RESULT's "What follows": the theme set is low-volatility alone; the horizon is 10 on a non-overlapping two-week grid; and the commit rule cannot select a construct an earlier campaign of the series already committed.

Not a construction test: the book is the short-tail book the last series registered, kept so that the one new lever on the book side is the horizon.

## Primary outcome (decided now)
Over the non-VOID campaigns, every committed pick scored as **one equal-weight portfolio of their short-tail books** over the 30 out-of-sample dates with `./run.sh portfolio-oos --picks <the series' picks>` under this config. **Pass** iff the portfolio's mean net 10-day return is > 0 **and** mean/sd·√30 ≥ 2.0 **and** at least 4 campaigns committed. **Fail** otherwise. One computation; the first complete run after the tenth close-out is the verdict. A series stopped before the tenth close-out for any reason other than a measurement defect is scored on the campaigns that closed out, with the 4-campaign floor still binding. Thirty dates is half the earlier series' sixty: the same t needs a per-period mean √2 larger relative to its sd, and that is accepted here as the price of the horizon.
