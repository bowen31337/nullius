# Addendum 1 to the M3 dreaming pre-registration

- **Filed:** 2026-10-17, about 07:15 UTC. No dreaming cycle has run (`policy_revision` holds 0 rows) and no M3 verdict exists. `2026-10-17-m3-dreaming.md` is unchanged, and its `.sha256` still verifies.
- **What changes:** the evaluation config criterion 1 is computed under, and where the baseline arm's scores come from.
- **What does not change:** the worlds, the incumbent, the abstention rule, the three criteria and their thresholds, and the one-verdict rule.

## The defect
The pre-registration names no evaluation config, and criterion 1, as implemented, pairs the winner's freshly scored financial rows against the 84 stored `m2-fixed` rows. Those stored rows are not comparable to a fresh score, for two reasons:

1. **Three different evaluation setups.** Each campaign's close-out scored its own committed pick under that campaign's own config. By the nodes' recorded provenance, the 84 campaigns span three setups: 42 on snapshot `540e9368` with cost model `522d691d`, 30 on `75d0a161` with `522d691d`, and 12 on `75d0a161` with `1e946e66`. A dreaming cycle scores every candidate under the one config its process holds.
2. **The old sandbox.** The stored rows were written from 2026-10-09 to 2026-10-14, before the sandbox's row-order fix (`c431753`, Addendum 2 of the shadow pre-registration). Each is a single draw.

Re-scoring the stored `m2-fixed` picks under today's research config gave relative differences of 80%, 555% and 111% on the first three campaigns measured, one with its sign flipped. Pairing a candidate against the stored rows would therefore measure config differences, not dreaming.

## The fix
- **One pinned config.** Criterion 1, and every financial-world score a dreaming cycle writes for M3, is computed under `~/.config/nullius/campaign/evaluation-config-archive-perp-bn.json`, sha256 `6adc9a93dd4b2b52ac82c4bab91bb8e3bc33af09b010b826dd7de73d12736425`. It is the research config in force (perpetuals, beta-neutral, biweekly, 30 out-of-sample dates from 2025-08-04), and the forward shadow and M4 trade the same instrument and book. The verdict records the config's sha256, and refuses if the file it is given hashes differently.
- **The baseline arm is re-scored, not read.** The baseline is `deploy/dreaming/incumbent.m2.py`, replayed under the pinned config and the current runtime. It commits the same node, or the same miss, as the stored `m2-fixed` row on 84 of 84 admitted campaigns (checked 2026-10-17 against `~/.local/share/nullius/research.db`). The stored `m2-fixed` rows stay as recorded history, and are used only for that pick-equivalence check.
- **Rows carry their config.** Financial-world rows written for M3 are filed under a policy version that names the config's sha256. The verdict pairs only rows filed under the pinned config's sha256, and refuses a pair scored under any other.

The pre-registration's requirement that the incumbent's score agree "to within 1e-12" now means agreement between two scorings under the pinned config and the current runtime, not agreement with a stored row from another config or runtime.

# Addendum 2 to the M3 dreaming pre-registration

- **Filed:** 2026-10-18, about 06:40 UTC. No dreaming cycle has run (`policy_revision` holds 0 rows), no financial-world row has been filed under a `#financial@` version, and no M3 verdict exists. `2026-10-17-m3-dreaming.md` is unchanged, and its `.sha256` still verifies.
- **What changes:** the cost model is pinned beside the evaluation config, and every financial-world row's version names it.
- **What does not change:** everything Addendum 1 left unchanged, and the pinned evaluation config itself.

## The defect
Addendum 1 pins the evaluation config by its sha256, but the cost model a score is netted through is not in that file. It is read from `NULLIUS_COST_MODEL_PATH`, and an unset variable means the shipped default, which is the **spot** schedule (`cost_model.yaml`, venue `binance_spot`, hash `522d691d…`). The pinned config prices a perpetual-futures book. By the nodes' recorded provenance, 72 of the 84 campaigns were scored with the spot default and 12 with `binance_usdm.yaml`. So two dreaming cycles run under the same pinned config could net their financial rows through different fee and funding schedules, and the row versions Addendum 1 defines could not tell them apart.

## The fix
- **One pinned cost model.** Every financial-world score written for M3 is netted through `packages/cost-model/src/cost_model/binance_usdm.yaml`, venue `binance_usdm`, cost-model hash `1e946e66e63e308c6d5258fdb8f1a610e7dab18cc2562a90d6084c719c5e958a`. It is the venue the pinned config's instrument trades, and the schedule the forward shadow already uses.
- **Rows carry both identities.** A financial-world row's policy version names the config's sha256 and the cost-model hash it was scored under. The verdict pairs only rows filed under both pinned values, and refuses, as Addendum 1 already requires for a missing row, when either arm lacks them.
- **The canary measures the same thing.** The evaluation canary was frozen on 2026-10-18 at 06:08 UTC under the pinned config with this cost model, on campaign `c6990362-aba1-4786-aeeb-142f17a92d88`, node `d7bb84d9-e2d9-5e61-98bf-386d07175c98` (score `0.0012316714031509238` for both halves), and `nullius-canary.service` sets `NULLIUS_COST_MODEL_PATH` to the same file.
